# Security

Gulpy holds the connection tokens of its users. We take reports seriously.

## Report a problem

Send an email to **security@trygulpy.com**. Do not open a public issue.

Include:

- what you found, and the steps to show it
- the version or commit
- your name, if you want credit

We reply in 3 business days. We tell you when the fix is out.

## Rules for testing

- Test only with your own accounts and your own copy of Gulpy.
- Do not read, change or delete the data of other people.
- Do not test the availability of the service (no load or denial-of-service tests).

## What an agent can never get

- The token of a connection. Gulpy keeps each token encrypted and makes the call itself.
- A tool or a permission that the user did not approve for that agent.
